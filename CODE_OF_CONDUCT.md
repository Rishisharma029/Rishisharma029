# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect our community members to be respectful, inclusive, and professional in their interactions.

## Our Standards

### ✅ Expected Behavior

- 🤝 **Be Respectful** - Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- 🎯 **Be Inclusive** - Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- 💬 **Communicate Clearly** - Express ideas constructively and listen actively
- 🔄 **Be Collaborative** - Work together toward common goals
- 📚 **Be Helpful** - Share knowledge and help others grow
- 🎨 **Be Creative** - Encourage innovation and new ideas
- 🔒 **Respect Privacy** - Keep personal information confidential

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- 🚫 Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- 🚫 Offensive comments related to gender, race, religion, or identity
- 🚫 Threats, violence, or hostile language
- 🚫 Unwanted sexual advances or attention
- 🚫 Sharing others' private information without consent
- 🚫 Spam, trolling, or disruptive behavior
- 🚫 Any form of abuse or toxicity

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Community events and spaces
- Official social media accounts
- Any representation of the community

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing [rishisharma.bca25@satyug.edu.in](mailto:rishisharma.bca25@satyug.edu.in) with:

- Description of the incident
- People involved
- Context and any relevant links
- How it affected you

All reports will be:
- ✅ Kept confidential
- ✅ Treated seriously
- ✅ Investigated promptly

### Response

Upon receiving a report, I will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt within 24 hours
2. **Investigate** the matter thoroughly
3. **Consult** with relevant parties if needed
4. **Determine** appropriate action
5. **Communicate** resolution to all parties

### Consequences

Consequences for violation may include:
- Warning or reprimand
- Temporary muting or suspension
- Permanent ban from the community
- Other measures deemed appropriate

## Appeals

If you believe action taken was unjust, you may appeal by emailing [rishisharma.bca25@satyug.edu.in](mailto:rishisharma.bca25@satyug.edu.in) with details and context.
