### 💫 About Me

I'm a BCA student who enjoys building software that is useful, experimental, and sometimes slightly unhinged. 

Most of my time goes into turning ideas into real projects instead of leaving them as notes or unfinished plans. I'm particularly interested in AI-powered applications, developer tools, web development, and systems that solve practical problems.

Outside of coding, you'll usually find me researching new ideas, improving existing projects, or adding features that absolutely did not need to exist but seemed funny at 2 AM.
